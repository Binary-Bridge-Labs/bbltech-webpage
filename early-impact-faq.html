常见问题

What is Early Impact AI Academy?

Early Impact AI Academy is Codeway's program that aims to attract exceptional AI talents. Early Impact AI Academy offers new grads, master's, and PhD students a tailor-made way to shine their careers in the mobile industry. For more information, please visit codeway.co/earlyimpact

Do you take applications from outside of Istanbul?

我们接受来自世界各地的申请。但是，为了为您打造最佳的 Early Impact AI Academy 体验并确保快速适应，我们希望您与我们一起在位于莱文特的伊斯坦布尔办公室工作。不过，在特殊情况下，我们也提供远程工作选项。

I am still studying my 2nd or 3rd year at the university, can I still apply?

很遗憾，不行。该项目仅对应届毕业生、硕士生和博士生开放。我们希望在未来几年见到你！

It has been 2-3 years since I graduated. Would you take my application into consideration?

是的！Early Impact AI Academy 接受新毕业生、硕士生和博士生的申请。

Is this an opportunity to join Codeway only for a limited time?

No, this program is a part of our regular hiring efforts. Youâll become a part of the team from the moment we shake hands. There is not a predetermined ending period for the Early Impact AI Academy hires.

Does the Early Impact program offer a salary or side benefits?

是的，Early Impact 提供有竞争力的薪酬。而且，您还将获得 Codeway 会员享有的所有附带福利，这些福利也在计划说明中列出。

Am I Going to Take Courses on AI?

我们相信学习的最佳方法是实践。您不需要参加传统的课程。但是，您将有机会在实际项目中学习很多东西，并且 Codeway 会为您选择的任何培训提供全力支持。

In Which Categories You Develop Mobile Apps?

我们开发的应用主要涉及照片/视频和娱乐类别。不过，我们欢迎您提出任何有前途的想法。

Are You Looking for Someone to Work on the Client-Side or Someone to do Research?

两者兼而有之！我们相信，灵活应对项目的不同方面是我们强大的原因。您既可以做一些研究，也可以参与您正在开展的项目的业务方面。您也可以专注于您选择的领域。
